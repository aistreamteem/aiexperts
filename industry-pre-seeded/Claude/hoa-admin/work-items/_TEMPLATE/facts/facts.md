# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context specific to this association job (this meeting, application, lot, aging as-of date, or bid project). They inform the pack but are not prescriptive.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

Work Item-level facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this Work Item's registered job.

Expert Role facts live in `facts/facts-operations.md`.
