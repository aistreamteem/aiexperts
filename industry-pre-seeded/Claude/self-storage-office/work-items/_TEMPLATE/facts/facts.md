# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations, data, and context specific to this self-storage-office Work Item (this unit, packet, occupancy date, late pull, or auction file). They are informative, not prescriptive.

**Format:** see `facts/MEMORY-INDEX.md` (use `### YYYY-MM-DD: Title`).

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD: Label/Title
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** operator, file name, or chat
**Status:** active

Content with full formatting preserved
```

Work Item-level facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit the Work Item's workflow context.

Expert Role-level facts go in `facts/facts-operations.md` with `**Workflows:** all` or a registry id.
