# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context specific to this clinic role job (this day, member, class, or pack). They inform the list or close pack. They are not club-wide until promoted.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional: export name, operator, path]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]

---
```

**Note:** Work Item-level facts do NOT need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this Work Item's role job.

For Expert Role facts use `facts/facts-shared.md` (`Workflows: all`) or `facts/facts-operations.md` with a registered job id.

---
