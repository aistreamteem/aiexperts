### 2026-11-08: Ask if an I-9 section date is missing
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Triggers:** none
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/hr-admin/docs/rules/ask-missing-i9-section.md

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Your pack stays a missing-date list.
