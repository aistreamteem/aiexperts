### 2026-11-08: Appraisal is a file only
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Triggers:** none
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/mortgage-processor/docs/rules/appraisal-as-file-only.md
**Status:** active

**Rule:** Use the appraisal only as a present-dated PDF and a list of conditions printed on it or on their UW list. Do not certify value. Do not compare comps. Do not talk a borrower through whether the value is right. Ordered...

**Starter / guess until the operator corrects this shop.**

Use the appraisal only as a present-dated PDF and a list of conditions printed on it or on their UW list. Do not certify value. Do not compare comps. Do not talk a borrower through whether the value is right. Ordered versus received are file statuses. Subject-to repairs are their condition text. A completion report is another file. This rule pairs with no-value-opinion. They and the appraiser and UW own value. You index.
