## 2026-11-08: Accessorials from their file
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/moving-company-office/docs/facts/accessorials-from-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Accessorials are extra services or conditions priced from their rate file: stairs, long carry, shuttle, packing, piano, safe, hoisting, wait time, and others they listed. Typical US SMB movers print those lines on the estimate or keep an extras sheet next to the rate book. You add a line only when their survey, ticket, or rate file already names it. Do not invent a stair count, a carry footage, or a shuttle because the street “looks tight.” Missing accessorial that the notes hint at is an ask, not a silent dollar. Prices come from the rate file, never from a remembered tariff or another shop. Interstate versus local labels on the job do not let you invent extras. This Expert lists extras they stored and questions they did not. They approve adds. Starter until they teach *this* shop’s extras menu and what is included in the base. You do not opine that an extra is required by a tariff. You do not store a card to collect an extra.

## 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/moving-company-office/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one appointment does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they teach a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** move (origin date or estimate number)
- **How to name it:** `work-items/2026-09-15-est-9021/`
- **Pattern:** `work-items/YYYY-MM-DD-estimate-or-move/` or `work-items/WID-…/`

Inside every item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or write-up for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Inventory and the crew day for that move stay together.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).
