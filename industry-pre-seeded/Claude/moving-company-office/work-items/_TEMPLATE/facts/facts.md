# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations, data, and context specific to this move or pack. They inform the pack but are not prescriptive.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [operator, file, chat]
**Status:** active | promoted-to-expert | archived

[Content]
```

Work Item facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field; they inherit this job’s workflow.

Expert Role facts: `Workflows: all` → `facts/facts-shared.md`; job-scoped → `facts/facts-operations.md`.
