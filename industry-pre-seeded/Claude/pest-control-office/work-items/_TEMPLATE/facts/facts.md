# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context for this Work Item (this route day, ticket pack, or named pack). They inform the pack but are not prescriptive.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional: operator, route CSV, ticket sheet]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

Work Item-level facts do NOT need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this pest-office job.

For Expert Role-level facts use `facts/facts-operations.md` with `**Workflows:**` (`all` or a job id).
