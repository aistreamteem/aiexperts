# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context for this Work Item (stay date, reservation, group, or pack). They are not prescriptive.

**Format:**

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD: Label/Title

**Scope:** work-item

**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD

**Source:** operator name, export filename, or chat

**Status:** active | promoted-to-expert | archived

Content with full formatting preserved.
```

Work Item-level facts do not need `**Workflows:**` — they inherit the Work Item’s workflow.

For Expert Role facts use `facts/facts-operations.md` with `**Workflows:** all` or a registry id.
