**Workflows:** all

**Rule:** If cutoff is blank on the group file, ask. Do not invent a 14-day or 30-day habit. Do not release unsold block rooms in a pack. Past-cutoff holds still on the sheet are flags. Late pickup at the group rate needs their...

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If cutoff is blank on the group file, ask. Do not invent a 14-day or 30-day habit. Do not release unsold block rooms in a pack. Past-cutoff holds still on the sheet are flags.
