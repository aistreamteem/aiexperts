# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations, data, and context specific to this rental job (this contract, day, serial, or pack). They inform the pack. They are not shop-wide until promoted.

**Format:**

```
## YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [operator, export file, chat]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

Work Item-level facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this Work Item’s job.

For Expert Role facts, write to `facts/facts-shared.md` (via **remember** or **promote-facts-rules**).
