## 2026-11-08: Add-on services
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/salon-front-role/docs/facts/add-on.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Gloss, trim, brow, toner, treatment, or paraffin add-ons change duration and price. Take add-ons from the ticket or menu file, not from a guess that “color guests always get a gloss.” Roles keep add-ons as separate lines so the book’s minutes and the POS dollars match. If checkout added a service the morning book did not have, list the mismatch; do not back-fill the morning book as if it had been there all day. Typical files are the POS ticket export and the menu item list. Upsell talk is theirs at the chair; your pack only records what was sold or booked. Do not add a complimentary add-on in the write-up to soothe a complaint. If an add-on has no menu name, ask—do not invent a SKU. Starter until they teach which add-ons this floor books versus which are sold only at checkout.

## 2026-11-08: The book
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/salon-front-role/docs/facts/book.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

The book is the floor’s chair map for a day: who sits where, with which named service, at what start, on which stylist. Typical US hair, nail, and beauty roles keep it so the next guest is visible, so color processing does not collide with a cut, and so later close-out can match tickets to appointments. Most shops export it from Vagaro, Boulevard, Square Appointments, Fresha, Booksy, or a photographed paper book. Typical columns are start time, the guest label the shop already uses, service or menu name, stylist or chair, duration or end time, and a status such as booked, confirmed, arrived, or completed. Duration and processing buffers usually live in the service setup, not as a free-text guess you type into the pack. Some floors also reserve shampoo bowls or private rooms as resources on the same export. If two names share a chair at the same minute, flag the clash and ask; do not move a guest to another stylist to “fix” the book. Online widgets and phone requests are not the book until their process confirms the row. Starter only until they teach how this floor labels guests and chairs.
