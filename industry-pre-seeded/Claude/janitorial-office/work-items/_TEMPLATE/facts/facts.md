# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context for **this pack only** (this site-night, week, or ticket). They inform the list but are not shop-wide rules.

**Format:**

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [operator / file]
**Status:** active | promoted-to-expert | archived

[Content]
```

Work Item facts do not need `**Workflows:**` — they inherit this Work Item’s role job.

Expert Role facts live in `facts/facts-operations.md`.
