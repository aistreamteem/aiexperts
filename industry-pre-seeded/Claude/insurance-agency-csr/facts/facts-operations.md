## 2026-11-08: ACORD completeness
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/insurance-agency-csr/docs/facts/acord-completeness.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they correct the starter.**

ACORD completeness is a present-or-missing check of the ACORD applications, certificates, and loss-notice forms they already use — not a rewrite of ACORD's instruction guide and not a license to invent answers. Typical US SMB agencies print ACORD 125/126 for commercial, 80/90 series for personal, 25 for liability certificates, 24 or 27 for property evidence, and 2 or 3 for auto ID cards, plus whatever their AMS library includes. You list form number, named insured as written, and which required boxes or attachments are blank on the PDF they dropped. Blank FEIN, missing schedule, unsigned producer block, or no prior-carrier section is a question, not a guess you fill. You do not complete underwriting questions from memory. You do not say the form is “good enough to bind.” They or the producer supply the missing answers. Starter until they correct the starter which ACORD set *this* agency uses per line and which blanks they treat as blockers.

**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/insurance-agency-csr/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they correct the starter.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one certificate request does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they remember a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** client + policy or certificate request
- **How to name it:** `work-items/WID-…/` or a pack id they use (`2026-09-15-coi-acme-holder`)
- **Pattern:** one Work Item per client + policy or certificate request

Inside every Work Item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or pack summary for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Expiration lists can be one named list folder. Do not mix two COI holders in one request folder.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).

**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/insurance-agency-csr/docs/facts/workers-comp-evidence.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they correct the starter.**

Workers-compensation evidence is their form or state-specific certificate, not a GL ACORD 25 reused as if it were WC. Typical US SMB contractors need WC shown to a GC. You copy carrier, policy number, and dates from the WC dec or AMS. You do not invent a WC limit or a sole-proprietor exclusion. You do not say employees are covered. Waiver of subrogation on WC is a separate form fact. Extra-territorial or other-states labels stay as printed. If they have no WC policy on the list, say missing — do not draft a fake cert. State bureau numbers are labels from their file. They issue the evidence. Starter until they correct the starter which WC form *this* agency prints and how they handle owners officers who are excluded on the dec. Experience-mod worksheets are a different file and not a number you invent on the certificate. You do not say a statutory limit means employees are covered. Missing WC on a contractor who asked for a job cert is a missing-policy row, not a GL substitute.
