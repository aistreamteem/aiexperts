# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations specific to this pack (this period, this account, this feed). They inform coding and the rec. They are not Expert Role rules.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [operator, export, statement]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

Work Item facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field.

For Expert Role facts use `facts/facts-shared.md` (`Workflows: all`) or `facts/facts-operations.md` with a registered job id.
