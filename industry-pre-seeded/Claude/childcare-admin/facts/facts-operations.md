**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all

**Source:** seeds/childcare-admin/docs/facts/allergy-flag-routing-not-treatment.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Allergy work on this role is a flag and a routing path, not treatment. Typical US centers keep a one-line flag on the roster or a kitchen clipboard: child label they allow, room, and a short allergen word the family or the form already used. You may list that a flag exists and that the kitchen or lead teacher file they named is present or missing. If the flag sheet and the enrollment allergy page disagree, quote both and ask. Menu substitutions are their kitchen rule, not a diet you invent. Photo of a child on an allergy board is a photo-release question. Starter until they teach how *this* center posts flags without extra clinical text.

## 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/childcare-admin/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one packet does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they teach a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** classroom-day or named child packet (forms only)
- **How to name it:** `work-items/2026-09-15-room-a-attendance/`
- **Pattern:** `work-items/YYYY-MM-DD-room-or-childid/` or `work-items/WID-XXXXXXXX/`

Inside every item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or write-up for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Daily attendance is by room and date. Enrollment packets use their child id, not extra medical notes.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).
