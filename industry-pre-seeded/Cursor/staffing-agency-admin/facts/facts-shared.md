### 2026-11-08: Assignment
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/staffing-agency-admin/docs/facts/assignment.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An assignment is one person on one client job order for a dated window: employee label, client, site, shift, start, expected end if they wrote one, and the rate pointers they already filed. Typical US SMB staffing offices print an assignment sheet or export a placement row from the branch system. You list the assignment as they coded it. You do not invent a placement to hide an open order. You do not extend an assignment past their end date. You do not fire the person off the assignment as counsel. A cancelled assignment stays cancelled if they coded it. Replacements are a new row or a linked row as they already do — you do not silently swap names. This is the agency placement file, not an internal HR job title for the agency's own office staff. This Expert builds the assignment board. They dispatch and they end assignments. Starter until they teach how *this* shop numbers assignments versus orders and what status words they allow.

### 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/staffing-agency-admin/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **Work Item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one assignment week does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they correct a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** assignment or timesheet week + worker id
- **How to name it:** `work-items/WID-20260915/` (or a pack they named)
- **Pattern:** `work-items/<id>/`

Inside every Work Item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or pack for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Use their worker number, not SSN. A week’s timesheet batch can be one named folder.
