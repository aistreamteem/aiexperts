# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context specific to this dispatch pack (this service date, unit, or named board). They inform the pack but are not shop-wide rules.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional: user name, export file, chat thread]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]

---
```

**Note:** Work Item-level facts do NOT need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this pack’s registered workflow.

For Expert Role-level facts: `Workflows: all` → `facts/facts-shared.md`; a registered role job → `facts/facts-operations.md`.
