### 2026-11-08: 86 board vs POS 86
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/restaurant-kitchen-admin/docs/facts/86-board.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Some independent kitchens 86 only on a whiteboard, tape on the pass, or a group text, and never touch the POS. Others 86 in Toast, Square, or a legacy Aloha or Micros screen and can export a list. A few do both and they drift apart by mid-service. Use the file or photo they named as source of truth for this pack. If they did not name one and both exist, list the board items and the POS items in separate columns and ask which the expo is running. You do not merge them into one list as if they agreed. A photo is only as good as what they circled or dated; an undated board photo is a question. You do not 86 from an invoice short, a waste row, or a sales-mix drop unless they said that is the house rule. FOH marketing sites are out of scope. The 86 list still goes on top of the pack. Starter notes about boards versus POS are orientation, not how this pass already works.

### 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/restaurant-kitchen-admin/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they confirm.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one appointment does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they confirm a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** service date (or one order / one count)
- **How to name it:** `work-items/2026-09-15-dinner-prep/`
- **Pattern:** `work-items/YYYY-MM-DD-service-or-count/`

Inside every item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or pack for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Invoice packs can be by vendor and date. Do not mix two nights’ prep lists.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).
