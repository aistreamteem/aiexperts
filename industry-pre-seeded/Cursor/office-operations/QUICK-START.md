# Role Expert — Office and operations

This folder is a role role Expert. You do not need to be a programmer.

1. Open **this** folder (the one with `MyRole.txt` and `AGENTS.md`). Do not open a parent folder that contains many Experts.
2. Read `MyRole.txt` for scope and must-never.
3. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `.cursor/rules/expert.mdc`.
4. Shop facts/rules live under `facts/` (operations memory). Workflows live under `.cursor/workflows/`. Tools live under `docs/tools/`. Method: `docs/role/work-item-method.md`.

## Give it one small real job

1. In chat, name the job (this week’s pack, this meeting, this mail log, this supply count). It should use a Work Item under `work-items/<id>/` so this work does not mix with the last job.
2. Put a file you already use (calendar export, tracker, log sheet, PDF) in that Work Item’s `in/` folder, or drop it here and say which week, meeting, or period it belongs to.
3. Ask for the work. Example: “Here is this week’s calendar export and task sheet. Make the weekly pack. Do not invent meetings or owners that are not in the file.”
4. When it finishes, look in **that Work Item’s** `out/` folder.

Starter notes already in `facts/` are typical for a US small office, **not** how *your* shop works until you say so.

## When it is wrong, tell it

- “We call that a pack, not a report.”
- “Never put full Social Security numbers in these lists.”
- “The min quantity is in our supply file. Do not invent one.”

Say **remember this** so it stores the correction. A few real jobs plus a few corrections is enough to get going.

## Do not

- Put passwords, bank logins, or card numbers in this folder.
- Ask it to send money or pay a bill.
- Ask it to be a lawyer, doctor, or CPA. Paperwork only. No legal stamp, diagnosis, or tax opinion.

## Back up this folder

Copy the **whole folder** to a safe place on a regular schedule. The notes about how your shop works, plus each Work Item, are the memory.
