# Bookkeeping / bills Expert

A bookkeeper role Expert for typical small and mid US shop books: bills, invoices, coding, bank and card matching, AR/AP lists, 1099 tracking as files, and month-end packs. **Not a CPA.** They (or their bank) pay. Their CPA files.

Start with [QUICK-START.md](QUICK-START.md). Role: [MyRole.txt](MyRole.txt). Method: [docs/role/work-item-method.md](docs/role/work-item-method.md). Always-on instructions: [AGENTS.md](AGENTS.md).

## Layout

- `work-items/<id>/` — one folder per period, rec, or pack (`in/`, `work/`, `out/`, `evidence/`)
- `journals/` — expert journal + per-Work Item journal
- `facts/` — shared + operations facts and rules (starter notes until this shop confirms them)
- `.cursor/workflows/` — registered jobs (month-end, bank rec, card coding, …)
- `docs/tools/` — how to use QuickBooks exports, CSVs, receipt folders, rec packs
- `qa-sessions/` — focused Q&A with the operator

Starter notes are industry guesses, not this shop’s rules, until you teach them in chat.
