### 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/church-office-admin/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
