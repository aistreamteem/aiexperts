# Role Expert — Event venue coordinator

This folder is a role Expert for **event venue coordinator** work: BEO packs, room holds versus definite bookings, renter-COI missing lists, load-in calendars, and F&B minimums copied from their file. You do not need to be a programmer.

1. Open **this** folder (the one with `MyRole.txt` and `AGENTS.md`).
2. Read `MyRole.txt` for scope and must-never.
3. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `.cursor/rules/expert.mdc`.
4. Shop facts/rules live under `facts/` (shared memory: `facts/facts-shared.md`, `facts/rules-shared.md`). Workflows live under `.cursor/workflows/`. Tools live under `docs/tools/`. Method: `docs/role/work-item-method.md`.

## Give it one small real job

1. In chat, name the job (this Saturday’s BEO pack, this week’s holds vs definite, this renter’s missing COI). It should use a Work Item under `work-items/<id>/` so this work does not mix with the last job.
2. Put a file you already use (BEO PDF, hold sheet, diagram, insurance folder, banquet-check PDF) in that Work Item’s `in/` folder, or drop it here and say which event date and room it belongs to.
3. Ask for the work. Example: “Here is this week’s hold sheet and definite book. Make the hold-versus-definite list. Do not invent a status that is not in the file.”
4. When it finishes, look in **that Work Item’s** `out/` folder.

Starter notes already in `facts/` are typical for a US banquet / venue role, **not** how *your* shop works until you say so.

## When it is wrong, tell it

- “We call that a definite, not a confirmation.”
- “Never put card numbers in these lists.”
- “The F&B minimum is in our contract file. Do not invent one.”

Say **remember this** so it stores the correction. A few real jobs plus a few corrections is enough to get going.

## Do not

- Put passwords, bank logins, or card numbers in this folder.
- Ask it to send money or pay a bill.
- Ask it to serve as the liquor license, stamp fire-marshal occupancy, invent a room rate, or give an ADA legal opinion.

## Back up this folder

Copy the **whole folder** to a safe place on a regular schedule. The notes about how your shop works, plus each Work Item, are the memory.
