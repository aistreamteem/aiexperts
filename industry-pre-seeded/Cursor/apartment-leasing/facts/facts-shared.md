### 2026-11-08: Accessibility request process
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/apartment-leasing/docs/facts/accessibility-request-process.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

An accessibility request is a resident or applicant ask for a change in rules, a physical modification, or an assistance animal, logged on their reasonable-accommodation form and routed to the person they named. Typical US conventional and affordable roles keep a short form and a log; they do not decide these at the leasing window as a legal call. You list that a form is present, missing, or still with the reviewer. You do not grant or deny the request. You do not decide that an animal is a pet versus an assistance animal. You do not invent a doctor's-note rule. You do not say the Fair Housing Act or ADA requires a particular ramp or stall. Parking and unit offers stay on the regular availability list without steering. This Expert routes the form. They and their reviewer decide. Starter until they teach *this* shop's form, log, and who may answer the applicant.

### 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/apartment-leasing/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one appointment does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they teach a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** guest card, showing, or application (one Work Item)
- **How to name it:** `work-items/WID-20260915/` (or the WID they give)
- **Pattern:** `work-items/WID-XXXXXXXX/`

Inside every Work Item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or write-up for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Prefer a guest-card or application number. Do not put two applicants in one folder.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).

**Captured:** 2026-11-08

**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A market waitlist is their ordered file of people waiting for a floorplan or a date, not a lottery you redesign and not an affordable-housing waitlist unless they labeled it that way. Typical US conventional communities keep a CRM waitlist or a spreadsheet with name, contact, floorplan wanted, desired move-in, and the date they joined. You list rows in their existing order. You do not jump a friend of the office to the top, and you do not drop someone because they have not called back unless their policy file says to expire stale rows. Affordable or income-restricted waitlists are a different file with their own rules; do not mix the two. A waitlist seat is not a unit hold and not an approval. When a unit comes available, they decide whom to call from the list. You prepare the call list from the file. You do not invent a position number. Starter until they teach how *this* shop orders, expires, and notifies waitlist rows.
