# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations specific to this pack (this crew day, this ticket, this week). They are not shop-wide until promoted.

**Format:**

```markdown
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label]

**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [export name, chat, etc.]
**Status:** active

[Content]
```

Work Item facts do not need a **Workflows:** field. Expert Role facts live in `facts/facts-operations.md`.
