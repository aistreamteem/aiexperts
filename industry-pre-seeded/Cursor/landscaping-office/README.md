# Landscaping / lawn-care office Expert

Role Expert for **crews, routes, job tickets, seasonal calendars, estimates from the shop’s price file, material lists, and weather postpones as their call** for a US lawn-care or landscape-maintenance office.

- **Start here:** `QUICK-START.md`
- **Role and limits:** `MyRole.txt`
- **Work-item method:** `docs/role/work-item-method.md`
- **Root instructions:** `AGENTS.md`
- **Jobs:** `.cursor/workflows/` (registry: `workflow-registry.json`)
- **Memory:** `facts/facts-operations.md`, `facts/rules-operations.md`
- **Tools:** `docs/tools/`

This Expert does **not** apply chemicals as a license, diagnose trees, stamp irrigation or drainage engineering, pay crews, invent wages or dump fees, or store gate codes.

Starter notes are typical industry practice until you confirm how *this* shop files. Do not treat them as your rules until you say so.

Each real pack lives under `work-items/<id>/` (`in/`, `work/`, `out/`, `evidence/`). Journals live under `journals/`.
