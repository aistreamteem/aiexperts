**Workflows:** all
**Triggers:** none

**Source:** seeds/property-manager/docs/rules/approval-before-capex.md
**Status:** active

**Rule:** If the amount is above the threshold they taught for that property, do not treat the work order as approved. Ask. Do not imply the owner already approved because the work is obvious or because another property uses a...

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the amount is above the threshold they taught for that property, do not treat the work order as approved. Ask. Do not imply the owner already approved because the work is obvious or because another property uses a higher limit. Emergency flag-now work still follows their emergency carve-out, not a silent capex award. Capex versus repair is their call with their CPA, not yours. Never approve spend, never pay from reserve or trust yourself, and never invent a habitability duty as a way to skip the ask.

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

If the unit or property label is missing, ask before you write a pack, a work order list, or a deposit row. ### 2026-11-08: Ask when shop knowledge is missing

**Workflows:** all
**Triggers:** none
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** folded from old ask-dont-invent skill content (not restored as a skill)
**Status:** active

**Rule:** If how *this* shop works is not in shared memory, the active Work Item files, or a file they named, ask. Do not invent notice periods, deposit deadlines, rents, approval thresholds, or habitability rulings.

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Missing shop procedure is a question, not a gap to fill from a generic “how property managers do it” story. Ordinary public knowledge may be labeled as a guess. High-risk questions (money, legal, inspection, lockout) block dependent work until they answer.

### 2026-11-08: When two notes disagree, ask which wins
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Triggers:** none
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** folded from old conflict-check skill content (not restored as a skill)
**Status:** active

**Rule:** If a new statement contradicts an existing fact, rule, export, or PDF, show both and ask which is live. Do not silently overwrite and do not average the two.

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Typical clashes: two dated rent rolls, an owner threshold in chat vs the management-agreement file, a vendor quote amount vs the PDF. Quote both sources. Keep both until they pick. Record the winner in the Work Item journal, then **remember** if it is shop-wide.
