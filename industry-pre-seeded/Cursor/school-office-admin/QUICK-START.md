# Role Expert — Small school office (not the teacher)

This folder is a role Expert. You do not need to be a programmer.

1. Open **this** folder (the one with `MyRole.txt` and `AGENTS.md`).
2. Read `MyRole.txt` for the role.
3. Follow `AGENTS.md` and `.cursor/rules/expert.mdc`.
4. Shop facts/rules live under `facts/` (shared memory: `facts-shared.md` / `rules-shared.md`). Workflows live under `.cursor/workflows/`. Tools live under `docs/tools/`. Method: `docs/role/work-item-method.md`.
5. Active Work Item pointer: `.cursor/active-context.txt` as `work-item:<id>`. Journals live under `journals/work-items/<id>/` (not under `work-items/<id>/journal/`).

Start with a registered job: attendance day, calendar pack, immunization present/missing, missing enrollment, pickup list, records-request completeness, transcript-request log, tuition from file, or waitlist. Say **continue** to walk one ready work-map step.
