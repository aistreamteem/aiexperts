# Working spreadsheet

**Pointer only. No passwords. Starter / guess until they confirm this yard.**

Typical US SMB roles live in Excel beside the rental system. Checks use csv-has-columns or file-exists as the step says. No password to a cloud sheet is stored here. This tool is the usual pack-out surface for packs they will read. Draft versus issued statuses stay as they coded them.
