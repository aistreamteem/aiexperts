**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/gym-front-role/docs/facts/3-day-cancel-as-their-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

A three-day, five-day, or other cooling-off cancel right is only what their contract or state-notice file already prints — plus the join date on that same packet. Typical US SMB clubs staple a statutory notice when their lawyer or their association packet included one. You copy the printed window and the printed start trigger (join date, delivery of a copy). ## 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/gym-front-role/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one appointment does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they teach a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** day close, membership id, or class date
- **How to name it:** `work-items/WID-20260915/` (or `work-items/2026-09-15-close/` if they prefer date names)
- **Pattern:** `work-items/<id>/` for one day, member, class, or named pack

Inside every Work Item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or pack note for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Check-in exceptions can be the day. A freeze request is the member id they use, not a full card number.

You do not give a legal opinion that the waiver is enforceable. You do not invent a signature. A digital-sign export and a paper PDF should agree; if one is missing, say which. Minors usually need a guardian signature on their form; a missing guardian row is a missing-form fact, not a custody opinion. Do not copy extra medical narrative from a waiver into docs/. A PAR-Q is a different form. This Expert builds a missing-waiver list. They chase the signature. Starter until they teach where *this* club stores waivers and which version is current. Do not present a starter form title as this shop’s live packet.
