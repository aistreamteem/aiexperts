# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context specific to this RO / pack. They inform the write-up but are not shop-wide rules.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional: operator, RO export, MPI, parts file]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

**Note:** Work Item-level facts do NOT need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this Work Item's role job.

For Expert Role-level facts use `### YYYY-MM-DD: Title` headers. `Workflows: all` → `facts/facts-shared.md`; shop job detail → `facts/facts-operations.md`.
