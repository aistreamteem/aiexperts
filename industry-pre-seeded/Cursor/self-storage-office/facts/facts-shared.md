### 2026-11-08: Abandoned as their file, not a legal label you apply
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/self-storage-office/docs/facts/abandoned-as-their-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Abandoned on this role means they already coded a unit or a leftover-goods file as abandoned, or they have an abandonment form in the folder. It is not a legal conclusion you apply to a quiet unit, and it is not an auction opinion. Typical US offices use abandonment for leftover boxes after a notified move-out, which may be a different path than a delinquency auction file. You copy their code and list the forms they named. You do not invent abandoned. You do not tell them they may throw goods away. Inventory and photo completeness still apply to their form. Cut-lock logs may sit beside the file. A quiet late unit is still a late-list row, not abandoned, unless they coded it. This Expert flags their abandoned rows and missing forms. They and their counsel pick the path. Starter until they teach how *this* shop distinguishes leftover-after-vacate from the auction-file path.

### 2026-11-08: How this role files work items
**Scope:** expert-role
**Workflows:** all
**Captured:** 2026-11-08
**Source:** seeds/self-storage-office/docs/facts/how-we-file.md
**Status:** active

**Starter / guess until they teach.**

Day-to-day work does **not** go in one shared pile. Each **work item** gets its own folder under `work-items/<id>/` so Tuesday does not overwrite Monday, and one unit does not mix with the next.

Typical name for *this* role (until they correct a different pattern):

- **What one folder is:** unit + move-in or auction file
- **How to name it:** `work-items/WID-20260915/` (or a shop id they already use)
- **Pattern:** `work-items/<id>/` with the unit/date in the journal description

Inside every Work Item folder:

- `in/` — files they dropped for *this* item only
- `work/` — scratch while this item is in progress
- `out/` — the finished list or pack for *this* item
- `evidence/` — check logs for this item only

Occupancy lists can be a facility-date folder. Do not mix two units.

If they already file another way (their software’s id, a property code, a period name), **use their words**. Ask before starting if it is unclear which item this is. Never dump new files into yesterday’s folder. Never put two appointments, two tickets, or two periods in the same item folder unless they asked for a combined pack and named it that way (for example one day-list).
