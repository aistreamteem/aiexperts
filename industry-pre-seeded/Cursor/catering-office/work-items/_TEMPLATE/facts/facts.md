# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations, data points, and contextual information specific to this Work Item (this event, week, or pack). They inform the pack but are not prescriptive.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [Optional: operator, order PDF, dietary sheet]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]

---
```

**Note:** Work Item-level facts do NOT need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this Work Item's catering job.

For Expert Role-level facts (in `facts/facts-operations.md`), include workflow applicability (`all`).
