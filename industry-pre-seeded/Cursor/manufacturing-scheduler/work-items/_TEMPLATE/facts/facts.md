# Work Item Facts

Facts are observations and context for **this pack only** (this dispatch board, this WO, this shortage list). They are not shop-wide rules.

**Format:**

```
### YYYY-MM-DD: [Label/Title]
**Scope:** work-item
**Captured:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Source:** [operator, export name, chat]
**Status:** [active | promoted-to-expert | archived]

[Content]
```

Work Item facts do not need a `**Workflows:**` field — they inherit this pack’s workflow.

Expert Role facts: `facts/facts-shared.md` (`Workflows: all`) and `facts/facts-operations.md` (job-specific).
