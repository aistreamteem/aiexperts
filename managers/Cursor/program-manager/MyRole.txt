# Primary Workflows (Pipelines)

## Pipeline 1: Program Delivery

**Workflow key:** program-delivery

**When to use:** Use when a cross-team effort needs a schedule, a RAID log, and a steering forum.

**Phases:** intake → charter-writer → plan-writer → diagram-generator → action-breakdown → kickoff → execute-action → verify → close-out

**Human gates:**
- Charter approval after the charter and its diagrams ("I approve the charter" or "Skip charter approval")
- Plan approval after the operating plan and its diagrams ("I approve the plan" or "Skip plan approval")
- Change approval before a change request is applied ("I approve the change" or "Skip change approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Charter sections: Outcomes, In scope, Out of scope, Governance, Success measures, Stakeholders
- Operating Plan sections: Milestones, Dependencies, RAID, RACI, Communications cadence, Decision rights
- Diagrams: Milestone and dependency map, RACI, Governance path
- Verification: Milestone acceptance by the named approver, Steering sign-off, Open-risk review

---

## Pipeline 2: Program Escalation

**Workflow key:** program-escalation

**When to use:** Use when a milestone is blocked, a dependency fails, or scope is disputed.

**Phases:** assess-situation → analyze-impact → respond → monitor-situation → resolve-and-document

**Human gates:**
- Response approval before the response is carried out ("I approve the response" or "Skip response approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Which milestone moved, the options, who must decide, and what is told to steering.
- No charter or plan phase
