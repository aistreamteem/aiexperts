# Assess situation

Write `docs/escalations/assessment.md`.

This escalation must cover: Who is affected, what decision is needed today, what is communicated, and what is watched next.

1. Ask what is known and what is still unknown. Unknowns that block the assessment are blocking questions.
2. Do not approve headcount or offers unless the operator explicitly approves that action.
3. Set next action to "run analyze-impact".
