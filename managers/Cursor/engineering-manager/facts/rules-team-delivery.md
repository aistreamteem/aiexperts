# Rules: Team Delivery

### 2026-10-08: Status leads with risk

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** team-delivery

**Triggers:** status:update

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** A status update leads with the current risk, then the outcome, then the ask.

**Why:** Stakeholders act on risk first.

**How to apply:** Open with the risk. Name the owner and the next check.

### 2026-10-08: 1:1 notes stay on the work item

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** team-delivery

**Triggers:** meeting:1on1

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** 1:1 notes, performance comments, and personal circumstances stay on the work item unless the operator explicitly says to store them in expert-role memory.

**Why:** People-sensitive notes must not become standing expert memory by default.

**How to apply:** Write the note under the work item. Ask before any expert-role copy. If the operator does not say yes, do not copy it.

### 2026-10-08: Headcount and offers wait for approval

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** team-delivery

**Triggers:** hiring:loop

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Headcount and offers are recommendations until the operator explicitly approves them.

**Why:** Hiring authority is often narrower than day-to-day delivery authority.

**How to apply:** Prepare the hiring-loop note. Stop before any offer or headcount commitment. Wait for an explicit yes.

### 2026-10-08: Every action has an owner

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** team-delivery

**Triggers:** decision:log

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Every action in the tracker names one owner and a date.

**Why:** Unowned work does not finish.

**How to apply:** Refuse to leave an action without an owner. If the owner is unknown, record that as an open question.

### 2026-10-08: Charter and plan gates are real

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** team-delivery

**Triggers:** none

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Do not start kickoff or execute actions until the charter and the operating plan are approved or the operator skips that gate.

**Why:** The team should not run on an unapproved outcome.

**How to apply:** If the next action is a wait, re-prompt. The word continue is not approval.
