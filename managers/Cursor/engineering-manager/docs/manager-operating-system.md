# Engineering Manager operating system

This expert supports one manager type. The folder is the type. define-role specializes the job, the workflows, and the skills. Facts and rules accumulate after that.

## Planned work

Use when a team objective needs an outcome, owners, and a cadence, such as a delivery commitment, a staffing plan, or a delegation change.

Phases: intake, charter-writer, plan-writer, diagram-generator, action-breakdown, kickoff, execute-action, verify, close-out.

The charter document is titled **Charter** and is stored at `docs/charter/charter.md`. The plan document is titled **Operating Plan** and is stored at `docs/plan/operating-plan.md`.

Charter sections: Outcome, Team scope, Success metrics, Staffing constraints, Non-goals, Stakeholders.

Plan sections: Milestones, Owners, Risks, 1:1 and hiring implications, Stakeholder cadence, Decisions needed.

Diagrams: Ownership map, Delivery timeline, RACI, Escalation path.

Execute actions produce: 1:1 agenda, Hiring-loop note, Delivery-risk update, Delegation note, Stakeholder status.

Verification checks: Success metrics checked, Stakeholder sign-off, Team-risk review.

Close-out: A status narrative that leads with risk, the decision log, and a handoff into the next cadence.

## Urgent work

Use when delivery slips, a people decision is urgent, or a production issue lands on the team.

Who is affected, what decision is needed today, what is communicated, and what is watched next.

## Change request

Use change-request for a change to scope, date, or staffing. Approval is required before the plan is edited.

## Ways of working

improve-ways-of-working cleans cadence, trackers, and decision hygiene without changing the agreed outcome.

## Memory

Load `facts/facts-team-delivery.md` and `facts/rules-team-delivery.md` for planned work. Load `facts/facts-team-escalation.md` and `facts/rules-team-escalation.md` for urgent work. `facts/MEMORY-INDEX.md` is the map.

## Role onboarding

define-role asks for the specific title, accountability, decision rights, a normal week, which workflows and skills apply, and any notes the operator already has. Confirmed notes become facts and rules. People-sensitive notes are stored in expert-role memory only when the operator says so.
