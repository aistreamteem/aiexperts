# Rules: Product Escalation

### 2026-10-08: Describe impact on the outcome

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** product-escalation

**Triggers:** metric:review

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The assessment states how the issue changes the outcome or the success metric.

**Why:** Urgency without outcome impact is noise.

**How to apply:** Name the metric and the direction of the change.

### 2026-10-08: Name the decider and the message

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** product-escalation

**Triggers:** stakeholder:update

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The impact note names who decides and what users or stakeholders are told.

**Why:** An undescribed message will be inconsistent.

**How to apply:** Write the decider and the message before response approval.

### 2026-10-08: Response approval comes first

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** product-escalation

**Triggers:** prioritization:decide

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Do not change priority or the public message until the operator approves the response.

**Why:** A launch problem still needs an explicit yes.

**How to apply:** Wait for I approve the response or Skip response approval.

### 2026-10-08: Do not pick up the build

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** product-escalation

**Triggers:** roadmap:update

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The response is a decision, a message, or a watch item, not a build task.

**Why:** Urgency does not change this expert's job.

**How to apply:** If the only next step is a build, record that as someone else's action and stop.

### 2026-10-08: Watch the metric after the decision

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** product-escalation

**Triggers:** metric:review

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Monitoring names the metric and the next reading.

**Why:** A launch decision needs a check.

**How to apply:** Record the metric and when it will be read again.
