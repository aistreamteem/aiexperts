# Primary Workflows (Pipelines)

## Pipeline 1: Product Initiative

**Workflow key:** product-initiative

**When to use:** Use when a user problem needs an outcome, a now-versus-later cut, and stakeholder alignment.

**Phases:** intake → charter-writer → plan-writer → diagram-generator → action-breakdown → kickoff → execute-action → verify → close-out

**Human gates:**
- Charter approval after the opportunity brief and its diagrams ("I approve the charter" or "Skip charter approval")
- Plan approval after the product plan and its diagrams ("I approve the plan" or "Skip plan approval")
- Change approval before a change request is applied ("I approve the change" or "Skip change approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Opportunity Brief sections: Problem, Users, Outcome, Non-goals, Assumptions
- Product Plan sections: Scope now, Scope later, Success metrics, Risks, Rollout narrative, Open decisions
- Diagrams: Journey, Prioritization, Stakeholder map, Decision flow
- Verification: Outcome criteria checked, Stakeholder sign-off, Unresolved-assumption review

---

## Pipeline 2: Product Escalation

**Workflow key:** product-escalation

**When to use:** Use for a launch problem, a priority conflict, or a user issue that changes the outcome.

**Phases:** assess-situation → analyze-impact → respond → monitor-situation → resolve-and-document

**Human gates:**
- Response approval before the response is carried out ("I approve the response" or "Skip response approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Impact on the outcome, the options, who decides, and what users or stakeholders are told.
- No charter or plan phase
