# Primary Workflows (Pipelines)

## Pipeline 1: Management Initiative

**Workflow key:** management-initiative

**When to use:** Use when an outcome needs owners, a cadence, decisions, and stakeholder communication.

**Phases:** intake → charter-writer → plan-writer → diagram-generator → action-breakdown → kickoff → execute-action → verify → close-out

**Human gates:**
- Charter approval after the charter and its diagrams ("I approve the charter" or "Skip charter approval")
- Plan approval after the operating plan and its diagrams ("I approve the plan" or "Skip plan approval")
- Change approval before a change request is applied ("I approve the change" or "Skip change approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Charter sections: Outcome, Scope, Stakeholders, Success, Constraints
- Operating Plan sections: Owners, Cadence, Decisions needed, Risks, Communications
- Diagrams: Stakeholder map, Operating cadence, RACI, Decision flow
- Verification: Success criteria checked, Stakeholder sign-off, Risk review

---

## Pipeline 2: Management Escalation

**Workflow key:** management-escalation

**When to use:** Use when a decision is stuck, a commitment is at risk, or stakeholders conflict.

**Phases:** assess-situation → analyze-impact → respond → monitor-situation → resolve-and-document

**Human gates:**
- Response approval before the response is carried out ("I approve the response" or "Skip response approval")

**Characteristics:**
- The stuck decision, the people affected, the options, and the next communication.
- No charter or plan phase
