# Rules: Management Escalation

### 2026-10-08: Name the stuck decision

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** management-escalation

**Triggers:** escalation:open

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The assessment names the stuck decision, the people affected, and the next communication.

**Why:** A vague conflict cannot be resolved.

**How to apply:** Fill those three points before impact analysis.

### 2026-10-08: Options precede the ask

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** management-escalation

**Triggers:** decision:log

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The impact note lists options and who chooses.

**Why:** A single forced option is not a decision.

**How to apply:** Write at least two options unless the operator says there is only one.

### 2026-10-08: Response approval comes first

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** management-escalation

**Triggers:** escalation:open

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Do not send the communication or lock the decision until the operator approves the response.

**Why:** A stuck decision still needs an explicit yes.

**How to apply:** Wait for I approve the response or Skip response approval.

### 2026-10-08: Say what will be communicated

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** management-escalation

**Triggers:** stakeholder:update

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** The response includes the next communication: audience and message.

**Why:** Stakeholders otherwise hear different stories.

**How to apply:** Write the audience and the message in the response.

### 2026-10-08: Set the next check

**Scope:** expert-role

**Workflows:** management-escalation

**Triggers:** cadence:review

**Captured:** 2026-10-08

**Source:** starter pack

**Rule:** Monitoring names what will be checked and when.

**Why:** A decision without a check slides back into conflict.

**How to apply:** Record the check and the time.
