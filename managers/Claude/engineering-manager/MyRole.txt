# Primary Workflows (Pipelines)

## Pipeline 1: Team Delivery

**Workflow key:** team-delivery

**When to use:** Use when a team objective needs an outcome, owners, and a cadence, such as a delivery commitment, a staffing plan, or a delegation change.

**Phases:** intake → charter-writer → plan-writer → diagram-generator → action-breakdown → kickoff → execute-action → verify → close-out

**Human gates:**
- Charter approval after the charter and its diagrams ("I approve the charter" or "Skip charter approval")
- Plan approval after the operating plan and its diagrams ("I approve the plan" or "Skip plan approval")
- Change approval before a change request is applied ("I approve the change" or "Skip change approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Charter sections: Outcome, Team scope, Success metrics, Staffing constraints, Non-goals, Stakeholders
- Operating Plan sections: Milestones, Owners, Risks, 1:1 and hiring implications, Stakeholder cadence, Decisions needed
- Diagrams: Ownership map, Delivery timeline, RACI, Escalation path
- Verification: Success metrics checked, Stakeholder sign-off, Team-risk review

---

## Pipeline 2: Team Escalation

**Workflow key:** team-escalation

**When to use:** Use when delivery slips, a people decision is urgent, or a production issue lands on the team.

**Phases:** assess-situation → analyze-impact → respond → monitor-situation → resolve-and-document

**Human gates:**
- Response approval before the response is carried out ("I approve the response" or "Skip response approval")

**Characteristics:**
- Who is affected, what decision is needed today, what is communicated, and what is watched next.
- No charter or plan phase
